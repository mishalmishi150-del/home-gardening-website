How to Care for Indoor Plants

Indoor plants make our homes fresh, healthy and beautiful.

1. Give Proper Water

Water your plants according to their needs.

2. Provide Enough Light

Keep your plants in a place where they get suitable sunlight.

3. Clean the Leaves

Clean plant leaves gently to remove dust and keep them healthy.
