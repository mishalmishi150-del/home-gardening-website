Gardening Tips for Beginners

Gardening is a great hobby. It makes our environment fresh and beautiful.

1. Choose the Right Plants

Choose plants according to your weather and available space.

2. Water Your Plants

Give your plants enough water to keep them healthy.

3. Provide Sunlight

Place your plants where they can get proper sunlight.
